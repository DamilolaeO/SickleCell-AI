## Published research
Research papers are used only when they meet all of these:
- **Open access with a reuse licence** (for example CC BY), so the text can be stored and quoted
- **High-level evidence:** systematic reviews, meta-analyses or large trials, not single small studies
- **Recent:** published in the last 10 years, unless it is a landmark study
- **Labelled as research** in answers ("Research suggests..."), never presented as medical advice
