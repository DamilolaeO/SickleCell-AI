# Intended purpose

Sicklewise is a free information tool for people living with sickle cell disease and the parents and carers who look after them. Children under 13 use it through a parent or carer's account. It is built in the UK first, and its education content is useful anywhere. It has two parts:

1. **An education assistant** that answers general questions about sickle cell disease in plain English. It answers only from trusted sources (such as the NHS, NICE, the Sickle Cell Society and WHO) and shows the source for every answer.
2. **A pain journal** that lets users record their own pain episodes and see simple summaries of what they recorded, for example to take to an appointment.

Sicklewise does **not** diagnose, triage, recommend treatment, give medication doses or interpret a user's own symptoms, test results or journal entries. It does not replace a doctor, haematology team or emergency services. When a user describes symptoms that may be an emergency, in an adult or a child, the app stops and directs them to urgent care: 999 or 111 in the UK, or local emergency services elsewhere.

This version is a prototype for education and demonstration. It has not been assessed as a medical device.
